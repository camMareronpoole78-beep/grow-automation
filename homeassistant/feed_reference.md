## Craft Blend soil
Living soil is designed to be self-sufficient — plain water alone can carry
a full crop start to finish. Everything below is supplemental, not required.
If you do feed: no more than once a week per product, and less is more —
don't stack everything on the same watering.
Source: https://buildasoil.com/pages/buildasoil-feeding-schedules

## Real Growers Recharge
**1/2 tsp per gallon of water, once a week.** Start as soon as roots are
visible, continue weekly through bloom, **stop 2 weeks before harvest**
(that's your flush window). Use within an hour of mixing.
Source: https://realgrowers.com/recharge/

## BuildASoil Vegan Bloom Booster (BuildABloom)
**Soil drench: 1 tsp per gallon of water.** Per BuildASoil's own schedule,
applied weekly during the bloom/fruiting phase only (not veg).
Sources: https://buildasoil.com/blogs/news/new-simplified-complete-buildasoil-schedule ,
https://hydrobuilder.com/products/build-a-soil-buildabloom-bloom-booster

## Watering volume — 20 gallon pots
General BuildASoil guidance is max 5-10% of soil volume per watering. For
20 gallon pots, that's **1-2 gallons per watering**. Small pot + big
plant → water more often (up to daily). Big pot + small plant → water less
often — at 20 gal with one plant each, that's the "less often" end, roughly
weekly.
Source: https://buildasoil.com/pages/watering-protocol-for-the-buildasoil-way

## Microbe Life Hydroponics Premium Vitamins & Amino Acids
**1-2 oz (30-60mL) per 10 gal of water, once or twice a week** — lean
toward the heavier end (2 oz, twice weekly) during active growth, lighter
during slower stretches. For smaller batches: ~1-1.5 tsp per gallon.
Shake well before use.
Sources: https://hydrobuilder.com/products/microbe-life-hydroponics-vitamin-amino-acids-32-oz ,
https://us.amazon.com/Microbe-Life-717610-Vitamins-Fertilizer/dp/B00CJJ2F2G

## TPS Plant Foods Worm Casting / Microbes + Humates
Read off the product label (Aug 2026).
**Add 1-2 tsp per gallon of feedwater**, applied via watering can, hose-end
sprayer (2 tsp setting), or leaf misting.
- **Spring & Summer: apply once weekly.**
- **Fall & Winter: apply every 4-8 weeks.**
- **Optional compost tea:** 4 oz Worm Tea + 1 oz molasses per gallon, brew
  with an air bubbler 1-3 days, dilute with fresh water before applying.
